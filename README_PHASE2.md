# Reflective Highway - Phase 2: Endless Driving

## Phase 2: Infinite Scrolling & Gameplay Logic

This update transforms the static scene into an endless driving experience.

### New Features
- **Endless Road Generation**: The world (Road, City, Lamps, Rails) generates procedurally in front of the player.
- **Cleanup System**: Old segments are destroyed to maintain performance.
- **Follow Camera**: Camera now chases the player sphere for a driving perspective.
- **Speed**: Player moves at constant speed (80 units/sec).

### How to Play
1. Launch the app.
2. The sphere will automatically drive forward.
3. Watch the city and lights generate infinitely!
